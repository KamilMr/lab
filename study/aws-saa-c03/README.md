# AWS Solutions Architect Associate — 60-day sprint

Public study notes for the [AWS Certified Solutions Architect Associate SAA-C03 course](https://www.udemy.com/course/aws-certified-solutions-architect-associate-saa-c03/).

## Study workflow

1. Open [the current position](progress/current-position.md).
2. Study the next lecture or topic.
3. Write an original summary in [notes](notes/README.md).
4. Record useful recall prompts in [Anki](anki/README.md).
5. Update the current position and mark the day in the [60-day sprint](progress/60-day-sprint.md).

## Daily duty

The daily study target is:

- **Monday–Tuesday:** 2 hours per day;
- **Wednesday–Sunday:** 4 hours per day;
- one short written note about what was learned; and
- updated course position and sprint status.

This gives a planned total of **24 study hours per week**. A sprint day is complete when its scheduled study time and the two documentation steps are complete.

## Structure

- [`progress/`](progress/) — daily completion and current course position
- [`notes/`](notes/README.md) — chapter and topic notes
- [`daily/`](daily/README.md) — optional daily reflections and study evidence
- [`anki/`](anki/README.md) — flashcards and card workflow
- [`images/`](images/README.md) — diagrams and screenshots safe to publish
- [`templates/`](templates/) — reusable note templates

## Sharing

The sprint table is the main shareable progress page. Keep notes safe for a public GitHub repository: remove account details and summarize course material in your own words rather than reproducing paid content.
